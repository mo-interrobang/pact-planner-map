How the score works

Points out of 100: grid access (30, distance to 345 kV+ substations and lines, HIFLD), planning capacity (20, townships and villages relying on outside or county planners score higher), Carlisle/Wortman as planner (10), named-developer proposals within 15 miles (15), a for-sale site marketed for data centers within 5 miles (5), and protection gap (20, no moratorium or ordinance scores highest; a moratorium that has lapsed or ends within 90 days scores nearly as high).

Check: towns that have already drawn a named developer outscore other towns about 81% of the time on the factors other than protection.

Moratorium status for 127 towns comes from a Nov 22, 2026 search of the mjbommar/moratorium-data-2026 inventory, datacenterbans.com, Michigan Public, Michigan Advance, Fox17 and Savrn. Many end dates are estimated from the adoption date and term, and some towns may have renewed since. Confirm with the township or city clerk before relying on any status.

This is a risk index built from public data, not a prediction. A high score is not evidence that any developer, planner or official is doing anything.
