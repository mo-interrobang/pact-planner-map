Metro Detroit Connections

How planners, lawyers, developers, utilities, regional development boards and land investors overlap across every data center fight in metro Detroit. Each community is fighting its own case. This page shows the threads between them.

No single person or firm touches every site. The overlaps stack up instead: CWA staff are the planning firm on 22 of 54 metro cases; one planner (Mike Auerbach) worked Allen Park and Saline Township at once; one developer partner (Sansone Group) shows up in Wixom and York Township; both federal moratorium suits sit with the same judge; and a regional site-marketing effort identified sites in Genoa, Huron and Van Buren townships years before those towns started writing rules. Developers and their counsel can see all of this at once. Individual townships see none of it.

PATTERN 1

One firm's staff cover nearly every tracked site

CWA/Carlisle-Wortman staff (Auerbach, Kreps, Masson-Minock, Lengyel, Passalacqua, Enos, Carlisle) are the planning firm on 22 of the 54 metro Detroit data center cases on the map, with different people at each site, not one individual.
