Steve Cassin — Multi-Municipality Watch CWA ALUMNUS

A dedicated companion page tracking planner Stephen "Steve" Cassin (Cassin Planning Group, LLC / CWA alumnus, joined Carlisle Wortman & Associates in 2017) across every Macomb–St. Clair County community where his name has surfaced: Port Huron Township, Casco Township, Lenox Township, Ira Township, Richmond, Richmond Township, Washington Township, and New Baltimore. Built after a report reached P.A.C.T. that Cassin has been influencing master plans across this corridor — every card below is labeled by how solid the evidence actually is. Hover a dot for detail; click to pin the card and open its source.

Compiled 2026-09-05 by P.A.C.T. from the statewide planner map, official township/city sites, master plan documents, and press. Amber = confirmed but not yet a documented problem. Teal = actively checked, no connection or no activity found. Blue (dashed) = unconfirmed, needs a primary source.
🟤 Confirmed — documented and sourced
Confirmed 1 — CWA Alumnus
Cassin joined Carlisle Wortman & Associates in June 2017
CWA's own announcement confirms Stephen Cassin joined CWA staff after 12 years as Executive Director of Macomb County's Planning & Economic Development Dept. and 42+ years total experience. CWA President Dick Carlisle personally welcomed him, noting it gave CWA four former presidents of the Michigan Association of Planning. Same lineage as Ben Carlisle, Auerbach, Lengyel, Passalacqua, and Elmiger. CWA announcement, Jun 23, 2017
Confirmed 2 — Port Huron Township
Cassin Planning Group is the township's planning consultant
2022 township documentation names Steve Cassin, AICP as planning consultant, with Karen LaVere also listed. No data center or battery storage activity found tied to the township.
Confirmed 3 — Casco Township (St. Clair Co.)
Cassin Planning Group credited on the 2024 Master Plan Update
The cover page of Casco Township's Master Plan (draft distributed Jul 15, 2024) credits Cassin Planning Group, LLC as the preparing firm. In-house ZA Joe Viaene appears to remain the day-to-day contact. The plan itself contains no data center, BESS, or new industrial-overlay language — see Checked-Clear below. 2024 Master Plan (draft)
Confirmed 4 — Lenox Township Data Bug
A month-old correction had never made it into the map's structured data
Lenox Township's notes have said since Aug 4, 2026 that the CWA planner there is Shana Lengyel, not Cassin — but the map's actual planner_name field still read "Stephen Cassin" until this session. Fixed 2026-09-05; the full misattribution history is kept in the record, not erased (see the Lenox lane below).

Unconfirmed 3 — Founding-Date Conflict

ZoomInfo lists Cassin Planning Group, LLC as founded 2014 — three years before CWA's 2017 announcement that he joined their staff. Either the LLC sat dormant while he worked at CWA, the founding date is wrong, or there's a gap in the public record about when he actually left CWA. No departure announcement found on CWA's own site.
🟢 Checked — no connection or no activity found
Clear 1 — Washington Township
Master plan is a Giffels Webster product, not Cassin's
The Township's Master Plan (Planning Commission Aug 8, 2024; Board Aug 28, 2024) credits Giffels Webster in its acknowledgments. Dana Berschback is the in-house Planning & Zoning Director. The township's real data center fight — Prologis's 312-acre proposal, withdrawn May 19, 2026 amid opposition — runs on its own timeline below, with no Cassin fingerprints found anywhere in it.
Clear 2 — Richmond (City)
In-house planner confirmed; no data center/BESS activity found
Troy S. Jeschke, Planning and Economic Development Director, is named directly on the city's own site. No outside consultant, and no data center or battery storage news found for the City of Richmond.
Clear 3 — Richmond Township
Volunteer-chaired Planning Commission, no paid consultant named
Paul Stewart chairs the Planning Commission as a volunteer; the township's own site names no paid Zoning Administrator or planning firm. No data center or battery storage news found tied to Richmond Township.
